# End-User Guides: Farmers & Buyers

This guide provides operational instructions for agricultural producers, grain elevator managers, and commodity buyers using the AgriResolve application.

---

## Participant Roles

AgriResolve supports two primary user roles during registration:

1. **Farmer / Aggregator**: The agricultural seller who produces, bags, or bulks grain, organizes transport, and delivers physical produce to the buyer facility.
2. **Buyer / Processor**: The commercial off-taker (mill, animal feed producer, or food manufacturer) who locks purchase collateral and receives physical grain at the weighbridge.

> Note: Arbiters are designated institutional grain inspection authorities or the protocol's automated AI Arbiter Oracle (`indexer`). General users cannot register as arbiters.

---

## Initial Setup & Digital Signature Key

AgriResolve uses the **Freighter** digital signature extension to authorize trade agreements.

1. **Install Freighter**: Install the Freighter browser extension from `https://www.freighter.app/` on Chrome, Firefox, or Brave.
2. **Switch to Testnet**:
   - Open Freighter settings.
   - Switch network selection from **Public** to **Testnet**.
   - Verify RPC is set to `https://soroban-testnet.stellar.org`.
3. **Fund Your Account**:
   - Copy your Stellar public address (starts with `G...`).
   - Fund your test address with testnet XLM using the Stellar Laboratory Friendbot at `https://laboratory.stellar.org/#account-creator?network=testnet`.
4. **Connect to AgriResolve**:
   - Navigate to the AgriResolve Web Application.
   - Click **Connect Wallet** in the top navigation bar.
   - Confirm connection in the Freighter popup.
   - Navigate to `/signup` and select your role (**Farmer** or **Buyer**).

## Guide for Buyers: Creating a Trade Escrow

Buyers protect their purchase capital by placing funds into escrow before trucks are dispatched from farms.

```
Buyer Workflow:
[Enter Contract Terms] ---> [Approve USDC Collateral] ---> [Deposit to Escrow] ---> [Escrow Status: FUNDED]
```

### Step 1: Initiate Trade
1. Navigate to the **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`).
2. Click **Create New Trade Agreement**.
3. Complete the required trade agreement fields:
   - **Farmer Address**: The Stellar address of your counterparty (e.g., `GCBOKYT7...`).
   - **Commodity Type**: Select standard commodity symbol (e.g., `MAIZE` or `SOY`).
   - **Contract Weight (kg)**: Physical grain weight (e.g., `10000`).
   - **Price Per Kilogram**: Unit purchase price (e.g., `0.35 USDC`).
   - **Total Escrow Amount**: Automatically calculated (e.g., `3500.00 USDC`).
   - **Delivery Deadline**: Maximum allowed delivery date and time.
4. Designated Settlement Token: Testnet USDC (`CCQGOTGN6HTQE74XYERYSYVU57T5NPYWV5DLCROMB56OXTV6RSO6KIGK`).

### Step 2: Sign Deposit Transaction
1. Click **Lock Escrow Collateral**.
2. Freighter opens a transaction approval dialog invoking `initialize_trade` on `agriresolve-escrow` (`CCPZFPNUQGNT5O6H3VWT2H23CLXF2POF5QYDAD4HPOB2RRKSHO63OIXC`).
3. Review parameters and click **Approve**.
4. The dashboard updates to display Escrow State **0: Funded**.

## Guide for Farmers: Dispatching Produce & Confirming Delivery

Farmers receive guaranteed payment protection once the buyer locks escrow collateral.

```
Farmer Workflow:
[Verify Escrow Funded] ---> [Load & Weigh Grain] ---> [Input Weighbridge Ticket] ---> [Escrow Status: DELIVERED]
```

### Step 1: Verify Collateral Lock
1. Open the **Dashboard** and locate the trade ID assigned by the buyer.
2. Confirm the stepper status displays **Funded**. Never dispatch grain if the trade status remains unconfirmed.

### Step 2: Dispatch and Submit Proof of Delivery
1. Weigh your truck at the certified departure weighbridge.
2. Transport grain to the buyer receiving silo or storage elevator.
3. Obtain the certified weighbridge delivery ticket (e.g., `Ticket #WB-2026-9812`).
4. On your AgriResolve Dashboard, select the active trade and click **Confirm Delivery**.
5. Enter the weighbridge ticket number and certified weight.
6. The application calculates the 32-byte SHA-256 hash of the delivery ticket.
7. Click **Submit Delivery Confirmation** and authorize the transaction via Freighter.
8. Escrow state transitions to **1: Delivered**.

## Delivery Acceptance (Happy Path)

When grain arrives within contract moisture and weight limits:
1. The receiving elevator samples the grain and confirms:
   - Delivered weight matches contract volume within tolerance (e.g. 10,000 kg ± 5.00%).
   - Moisture meter reports grain at or below base standard (e.g. 12.00%).
2. The buyer clicks **Accept Delivery & Release Escrow**.
3. The escrow contract disburses 100% of the collateral directly to the farmer address in 5 seconds.
4. Trade state updates to **3: Resolved**.

## Dispute Escalation & Evidence Upload (Unhappy Path)

If arriving grain fails quality standards, the buyer or farmer can trigger an automated audit.

```
Dispute Workflow:
[Flag Dispute] ---> [Upload Sensor/Scale Evidence] ---> [AI Arbiter Audits via Minary] ---> [Atomic Split Settlement]
```

### Step 1: Open Dispute Dossier
1. In the Dashboard, open the trade record and click **Raise Trade Dispute**.
2. Enter the dispute reason (e.g., `Excess Moisture: 16.00% vs 12.00% contractual base`).
3. Authorize the `raise_dispute` transaction on `agriresolve-dispute` (`CCBQBMQFQPRRMOQLPNYSIBGTL7AOXXQAAIADXE2YYTZHSUSDS6Z4ES5N`).
4. The escrow immediately locks funds. Neither party can withdraw collateral unilaterally.

### Step 2: Submit Digital Evidence
Use the built-in **Evidence Uploader** panel:
- **Evidence Type**:
  - `WEIGHT`: Weighbridge scale gross/tare tickets.
  - `MOISTURE`: Calibrated benchtop moisture meter readouts.
  - `QUALITY`: Laboratory certificates detailing foreign matter or damaged kernels.
- The web app generates a 32-byte hash for each attachment and calls `submit_evidence`.

### Step 3: Automated Audit and Split Settlement
1. The background Oracle service detects the dispute and ingests trade parameters and laboratory readings.
2. The engine computes the Minary shrinkage adjustment:
   - Excess moisture above 12.00% is converted into kilograms of lost dry matter.
   - Deduction basis points are calculated.
3. The Oracle signs `resolve_dispute` using its administrative key.
4. The smart contract executes token transfers simultaneously:
   - **Buyer** receives refund for lost water weight.
   - **Farmer** receives full payment for net delivered dry grain.
5. The dispute dossier closes with status **Resolved**.

## User Interface Reference

The AgriResolve web interface (`apps/web`) uses flat, functional UI components:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Navbar] AgriResolve | Active Network: Stellar Testnet | GCBOKYT7... (Connected)  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [EscrowStepper]                                                                   |
| (1) Funded  ===>  (2) Delivered  ===>  (3) Disputed  ===>  (4) Resolved           |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [TradeDetailsPanel]                                                               |
| Escrow ID: #1042           | Commodity: White Maize (MAIZE)                      |
| Contract Amount: 3,500 USDC| Quantity: 10,000 kg @ 0.35 USDC/kg                  |
| Base Moisture: 12.00%      | Delivery Tolerance: 5.00%                           |
| Buyer: GCBOKYT7...         | Farmer: GDX712...                                   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [EvidenceUploader]                                                                |
| Upload Weighbridge Ticket or Moisture Log -> Generates SHA-256 On-Chain Proof     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

- **Navbar**: Shows wallet connection status, network indicator, and active role.
- **EscrowStepper**: Five-stage visual status indicator reflecting on-chain contract state.
- **TradeDetailsPanel**: Summarizes physical trade parameters, commodity specifications, and dollar values.
- **EvidenceUploader**: Converts physical PDFs and sensor CSVs into SHA-256 digests and submits proofs to `agriresolve-dispute`.
